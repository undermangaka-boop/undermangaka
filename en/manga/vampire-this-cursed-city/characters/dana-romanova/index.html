Biography

Dana is a young vampire of Clan Toreador and a psychotherapist. She is accustomed to looking for the reasons behind people's actions instead of rushing to judgment. To her, the human mind is not a riddle to solve, but a world to understand. This quality only grew stronger after her Embrace. Her new life changed her nature, but not the way she sees those around her. Dana watches closely, analyzes, and is rarely wrong in her judgments of others. She understands that almost every decision, fear, or act of cruelty has a reason behind it, and this makes her one of the most perceptive Kindred in her circle.
